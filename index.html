Gya Labs: cart, rewards and automation for a DTC brand

Essential oils, US and European customers · Own the store's technical side end to end

11–42%month-on-month AOV lift in the months my releases shipped

Months with an increase. AOV also moves with marketing and pricing; these are the months this work shipped.

The store's offers had grown piece by piece: free shipping, free gifts, bottle discounts, subscriptions and Shipping Protection. They clashed. Gifts went missing, a $0 gift could be bought on its own, page and cart prices disagreed, and checkout sometimes stuck.

  • Reward ladder: one $50 free-shipping threshold plus free gifts at $75 and $95, with both gifts added in a single cart request so none is lost.
  • Loopholes closed: $0 gift variants redirect to the paid variant and never show a $0 price in cards or recommendations.
  • Prices that always match: a per-line bottle discount ladder (2 for 10%, 3+ for 15%, 6+ for 20%) that also applies on subscription renewals, rounding matched to Shopify's, and Shipping Protection kept out of gift thresholds.
  • New product page for 30+ oils: choose a use, then a bottle or a set, with a set upsell and a live rewards bar.
  • Cart pop-up: checkout waits until gifts and protection are in place, and shows the real total.
  • Fraud screening in Shopify Flow: orders scored on risk level, address mismatch, IP and country, order velocity, customer history and email patterns; risky orders are held, flagged to the team and cancelled if not confirmed in time.
  • Data and feeds: a Postgres to Shopify inventory sync with a review screen, Google Merchant Center disapprovals fixed at the root, structured data and AI-crawler readiness.

Result: customers get the gifts and free shipping they are promised, prices match from product page to checkout, fewer chargebacks with fraud caught before shipping, and average order value lifting in each month a release shipped.

ALOGIC: Magento 2 to Shopify Plus, 11 countries

I originally built ALOGIC's multi-store, multi-country Magento 2 site and ran it for three years. As the business grew, hosting costs and the work of Magento upgrades kept climbing, so it moved to Shopify. The move had to keep the catalogue, customer accounts and regional structure intact.

  • Planned and ran the migration solo, with regional stores on Shopify Plus and Advanced according to each market's needs. A second developer joined after launch.
  • Moved about 1,000 products, 5,000 customers and the CMS content with Matrixify and my own Node.js scripts on the Admin API.
  • Set up URL redirects from the old Magento URLs so search rankings and inbound links carried over.
  • Redesigned and built every product page template, the homepage and the product-launch landing pages.
  • Made product pages editable by the non-technical team, then supported the stores after launch.

Result: all 11 countries live on Shopify with rankings kept, sales up after the move, and page speed on par with an already fast Magento store.

Bytecart: 10,000+ products from a distributor, live in a month

US tech retailer in the ALOGIC group · Built from scratch, solo

1 monthfrom empty store to launch
  • Shopify
  • Ingram Micro API
  • Node.js
  • Admin API
  • Scheduled CSV

Bytecart wanted to sell a large catalogue straight from the distributor, Ingram Micro, without staff editing products by hand.

  • Built the store from scratch on Shopify, working alone from setup to launch.
  • Imported 10,000+ products through the Ingram Micro API and mapped them into a clean category and collection structure.
  • Designed a custom navigation so shoppers can browse a very large catalogue by category and by brand.
  • Wrote Node.js jobs that process the distributor's ~20 MB scheduled feed: stock syncs three times a day, prices weekly.

Result: a five-figure catalogue launched in about a month by one developer, with stock and prices kept in step with the distributor automatically.
